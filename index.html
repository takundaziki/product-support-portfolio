How I work

Support is not just closing the current case. The useful work is understanding what happened, communicating it well, and making the next occurrence easier to solve.

01 · Investigate

Follow evidence, not assumptions

I use ServiceNow histories, related incidents, sign-in and audit information, and system context to narrow problems before escalating.

02 · Communicate

Make technical problems understandable

I explain findings and next steps to users with different levels of technical confidence, while giving specialist teams the evidence they need.

03 · Improve

Turn repeated problems into reusable knowledge

I create knowledge articles and troubleshooting guidance from recurring issues, and share complex troubleshooting approaches with colleagues.

04 · Go deeper

Understand what sits behind the product

My projects span REST APIs, JSON, Azure Functions, Cosmos DB, JavaScript, PowerShell, Git and automated deployment workflows.

Support stories

Three examples of the kind of investigation, judgement and cross-team work I handle in a live enterprise environment.

Identity & access failures

Problem: users can present with MFA, lockout, Conditional Access or account-state symptoms that look similar but have different causes.

Approach: review Entra sign-in and audit information, verify the user and account context, isolate the likely cause, resolve within scope or escalate with useful evidence.

Credential compromise

Problem: a legitimate internal account was involved in a phishing incident using a SharePoint lure.

Approach: supported the investigation by reviewing sign-in evidence, escalating to Cyber Security, and helping coordinate account recovery and user follow-up under the revised access controls.

Security-sensitive supportSign-in evidenceCross-team response

Email delivery & access

Problem: email cases range from non-delivery and quarantine to mailbox permissions, forwarding, calendar access and rules.

Approach: use Exchange Online tools such as message trace and administrative controls to investigate the symptom, test likely causes and restore service or escalate appropriately.
